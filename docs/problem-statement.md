# Problem Statement: L1 — Container Congestion Predictor & Port Operations Optimiser

## 1. The Core Problem
Global maritime container ports handle over 80% of world merchandise trade. However, port operations management relies heavily on manual allocation of berths, quay cranes, and yard space across hundreds of arriving container vessels.

Key operational breakdowns include:
- **Reactive Congestion Detection**: Hotspots are discovered only after queues form, resulting in compounding delays.
- **Suboptimal Resource Utilization**: Quay cranes and berths are allocated statically, leading to idle capacity at one quay while another suffers from severe bottlenecking.
- **Delayed Rerouting Decisions**: Alternate routing recommendations (e.g. diverting to nearby regional ports or switching berth windows) come too late to prevent demurrage penalties and supply chain disruptions.

## 2. Affected Users & Stakeholders
- **Shift Supervisors & Port Controllers**: Struggle with manual schedule balancing across 12-hour shifts.
- **Terminal Operators**: Face high operational costs, labor overtime, and penalty fees for missing vessel turnaround windows.
- **Shipping Lines & Vessel Captains**: Suffer extended waiting times at anchorage, burning fuel and missing tight liner schedules.

## 3. Current System Limitations
Existing Terminal Operating Systems (TOS) store historical records but lack real-time predictive intelligence, constraint-based multi-variable optimization, and natural language copilot support for shift supervisors.

## 4. Impact of the Solution
Our solution provides real-time predictive visibility, automatic berth and crane conflict-free scheduling, synthetic alternate routing diversions, and a 72-hour hour-by-hour operational plan that reduces vessel turnaround waiting times by up to 42%.
